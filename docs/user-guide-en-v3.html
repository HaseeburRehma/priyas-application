1 · Welcome & who this guide is for

This guide is written for the person who runs the operations of Priya's Reinigungsservice day-to-day — assigning employees to customers, tracking hours, and making sure every visit is invoiced correctly. It covers both service lines:

Priya's regular Cleaning contracts

Homes and companies that pay directly. Invoices go out automatically through Lexware.

Alltagshilfe Care support (§45b SGB XI)

Elderly customers billed to the care fund. Invoices are issued manually by your team, one per calendar month.

What just changed. The 30 Alltagshelfer customers from Priya's spreadsheet have been imported, each customer now has a document file cabinet, a payer-type chip is shown on the customer list, and there is a tablet-friendly intake wizard that captures the paper Kunden-Erfassungsbogen digitally.

2 · Signing in & the dashboard

  1. Open the app at https://priyas-application.vercel.app in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Sign in with your work email address and password. First-time users receive an invitation email; click the link and set your password.
  3. Two-factor code. On your first sign-in you'll be asked to scan a QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or 1Password). Every future sign-in asks for a 6-digit code.
  4. Landing on the dashboard. You'll see four KPI tiles at the top, today's shifts in the middle, and the recent activity feed on the right.

3 · Roles and what each one can do

Every person in the system has one of five roles. The role decides what they can see and change.

Admin

  • Everything below, plus:
  • Adding & removing users
  • Editing organisation settings
  • Approving invoices
  • Deleting records

Dispatcher

  • Creating customers & properties
  • Uploading documents
  • Scheduling shifts
  • Approving hours
  • Generating invoice drafts

Field staff

  • Their own shifts
  • GPS check-in / -out
  • Damage reports
  • Team chat
  • Vacation requests

Auditor

  • Read-only access to everything
  • Cannot change any data
  • Used for Priya's monthly compliance review

Customer contact

  • Sees only their own visits
  • Downloads their monthly invoice
  • Rarely used yet — for the customer-portal roll-out later this year
Rule of thumb. If a colleague reports "I can't see the schedule button" — check their role first. Field staff intentionally see only their own shifts, not the org-wide schedule.

7 · Uploading client documents

Every customer now has a digital file cabinet — the paper folders your team keeps today can move into the app so anyone can find them in seconds.

  1. Open a customer. Scroll to the Documents card.
  2. Pick a category from the dropdown:
    • Contract — signed service agreement
    • Form — care-fund forms, tax forms
    • Decision — Pflegegradbescheid from the Krankenkasse
    • ID card — copy of the customer's ID or insurance card
    • Invoice — a copy of an outgoing bill
    • Photo — before / after photos, meter readings, key photos
    • Other — anything else
  3. Add an optional note — up to 80 characters.
  4. Click Upload file. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC, Word (doc / docx), Excel (xls / xlsx). Maximum 50 MB per file.
Privacy. Files are stored in a private storage bucket. The link that appears when you click Open is only valid for 60 seconds and cannot be forwarded — every viewer gets their own signed URL.

To view a document again later, click Open — it opens in a new tab. To remove one, click Delete (a manager permission).

8 · Adding properties (workplaces)

A property is the actual address where a visit happens. Most Alltagshilfe customers have a single property (their apartment). Companies often have several buildings under one contract.

  1. From the customer detail page, click Properties in the top tab bar, then Add property.
  2. Fill in:
    • Property name — e.g. "Onkenstraße 12 – EG links"
    • Address, PLZ, City
    • Kind — Apartment, House, Office, Commercial, Care home…
    • Weekly frequency — 1 for weekly, 0.5 for every two weeks
    • Access notes — key location, alarm code, parking, dog
  3. Save. The property is now bookable on the schedule.
Why properties matter. Shifts are always attached to a property, never to a customer directly. This means the app can show the field staff exactly where to go and, later, the GPS check-in verifies they arrived at the right address.

10 · Skills, qualifications, GPS check-in

On any employee's detail page you'll see three cards below the master data.

Skills & qualifications

Click Add skill. Common tags: window cleaning, industrial floor, car driver, bilingual DE/TR, bilingual DE/AR, certified Alltagshelfer §45a, Erste-Hilfe-Kurs. Skills feed the search box on the schedule — you can filter available staff by skill.

Training progress

Onboarding videos plus their completion state. New employees are locked out of scheduling until all mandatory videos are watched. You can waive individual videos with a manager note.

Documents

Same file cabinet as customers, scoped to the employee — contract, tax card (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung), Führungszeugnis, driver's licence copy, etc.

How GPS check-in works

  1. The employee opens the mobile app on their phone (Android or iPhone).
  2. They tap the day's shift → Clock in.
  3. The app captures their current GPS coordinates and compares them against the property's address.
  4. If they are within 300 metres, the shift starts. If not, the app asks them to confirm they are actually on site — the discrepancy is logged.
  5. Breaks and clock-out work the same way.

11 · Scheduling — the timeline

This is where you spend most of your day as an administrator.

  1. Click Schedule in the sidebar.
  2. The default view is the current week, employees down the left, days across the top. Each cell is one day.
  3. Switch view modes at the top:
    • Week — the operational planning view
    • Day — zoomed to today, with hourly detail
    • Month — bird's-eye view for spotting gaps
  4. Filter buttons above the grid narrow the list by service line (Priya's / Alltagshilfe), role, or skill.

Creating one shift

  1. Click an empty cell at the employee × day intersection you want. The dialog is titled Plan shift with the hint "Set property, staff member and time range."
  2. Fill:
    • Property — pick from the dropdown; only properties the chosen employee is qualified for appear
    • Employee — leave as-is for the row you clicked, or choose another (including — No employee (open) — if you're pre-booking)
    • Date, Start, End — the app validates that End is after Start
    • Notes — access, notes, special requests
  3. Click Create shift. The toast "Shift planned." confirms; the block appears on the grid — green for Priya's, red for Alltagshilfe.
  4. A push notification lands on the employee's phone; the shift also shows in their My schedule tab.

Editing or moving a shift

  • Click a shift block to open its detail — the same dialog appears with actions Reschedule shift, Reassign shift, Cancel shift, or Delete.
  • Drag a block to another cell to move it (the employee whose row it lands in becomes the new assignee); the toast reads "Shift moved." or "Shift reassigned."
  • Cancelling asks to confirm — the row stays visible for audit. Deleting asks a stronger confirmation because it cannot be undone.

What the dialog checks before saving

  • End must be after start — inline error otherwise.
  • Employee qualification — the property dropdown is filtered to properties the chosen employee is qualified for, with the hint "Pick a property {name} is qualified for."
  • If a save fails for any other reason (permission, network), the toast reads "Save failed." — the shift is not created and you keep your inputs.
Every shift is one row today. There is no series-builder yet — for a recurring visit, click the same time slot on each future date. A one-click "repeat every 2 weeks" flow is planned.

Worked example — booking Frau Hannelore Annies (Kundennummer 10008)

Say Frau Annies (Onkenstraße 12, 45144 Essen — one of the 30 imported Alltagshelfer customers) asks for a Tuesday morning visit every two weeks with Aylin from your care team. Here is the exact click-path:

  1. Verify the customer is in the list. Click Clients, filter by Alltagshilfe. Type "Annies" in the search — the row shows a red Alltagshilfe chip and (because she is private-pay) an amber Private pay chip.
  2. Confirm the property exists. Click her row → Properties tab. The import created one automatically at Onkenstraße 12; if you need a second (e.g. she moves), click Add property here first.
  3. Open the schedule. Click Schedule in the sidebar. In the top filter bar, select Alltagshilfe — only the 8 qualified care staff remain visible.
  4. Pick the intersection. Find Aylin's row and this coming Tuesday column. Click the empty cell.
  5. Fill the Plan shift dialog:
    • Property: pick Onkenstraße 12 from the Choose a property… dropdown (this is Frau Annies' address).
    • Employee: auto-fills to Aylin because you clicked her row.
    • Date, Start, End: Tuesday · 09:00 · 11:00 (a 2-hour visit is typical for Alltagshilfe).
    • Notes: anything for Aylin she can't guess from the property notes.
  6. Click Create shift. One red block appears on Aylin's row and the toast reads "Shift planned." A push notification lands on Aylin's phone; the shift also appears in her mobile My schedule.
  7. Recurring? For a fortnightly plan, repeat step 5 for each Tuesday you want to book (each shift is one saved row today; a repeating-series builder is on the roadmap).
What the app blocks — and why. If you pick a cleaning-only employee instead of Aylin, the save fails with "Only Alltagshilfe-qualified staff can be scheduled for this customer." The same guard runs in reverse: an Alltagshilfe employee can be booked for regular Priya visits, because being qualified for care doesn't disqualify them from cleaning work.

Booking a whole day for a regular Priya customer

The Priya's-side workflow is identical except for two things:

  • The customer chip on the row is green instead of red.
  • You often set a break — 30 minutes on a 6+ hour cleaning day for legal compliance. The break is deducted automatically from the billable total.

12 · Approving hours & generating invoices

End of every shift — Billing approval

When the employee clocks out, the shift lands on the Billing approval page (Schedule → Billing approval). The page title is "Billing approval" with the subtitle "Completed shifts still waiting for billing approval."

  1. Each row shows Property · Employee · Date · Scheduled · Actual · Billable (hrs).
  2. Compare Scheduled vs. Actual. If they match the plan, click Approve — the toast reads "Approved (X.X hrs)."
  3. If something is wrong, click Reject, fill the required reason and confirm — the shift returns to the employee for correction.
  4. When the list is empty you'll see "No shifts to approve. Completed shifts appear here automatically."
  5. Approved shifts are locked and ready to be billed. Use the button Go to "New invoice" → at the top of the page to walk one client through invoicing next.

End of every month — Priya's regular clients (Monthly Lexware billing)

  1. Click Invoices in the sidebar (or the Monthly Lexware billing page directly). Subtitle: "Generate monthly invoices from completed shifts and push them to Lexware Office."
  2. Click Preview month — a dry-run summary shows Clients to invoice, Total amount, Already invoiced, and any Errors.
  3. If the preview looks right, click Generate now, then Generate in the confirmation. The system creates one Draft invoice per client with approved hours that month.
  4. Review each draft. Use the top-right bulk button Lexware Sync to push all pending invoices — the toast reads "Pushed X to Lexware." The chip on synced rows turns Sent.
  5. Once Lexware reports payment back, the chip flips to Paid; anything unpaid after 14 days becomes Overdue.

End of every month — Alltagshilfe clients (manual insurer invoice)

  1. Open Reports → Alltagshilfe monthly report (breadcrumb: Overview → Reports → Alltagshilfe monthly report).
  2. The page runs automatically month-end and is titled "Monthly report" with the note "Hours delivered per Alltagshilfe client · ready for manual invoicing." The banner says Auto-generated.
  3. Use the report to create one manual invoice per insurer in Lexware and attach the report PDF/CSV per the email footer instructions: "1) Review the report, 2) Create one invoice per insurance company manually in Lexware, 3) …"
Which colour means what? Grey = Draft (nothing sent yet). Amber = Sent (waiting for payment). Green = Paid. Red = Overdue (14 days after sent date).

13 · Chat, notifications & mobile app

Team chat

Click Chat in the sidebar. There's one #general channel plus channels per customer and per property. Post a message once, everyone with access to that scope sees it.

Notifications

The bell icon top-right shows all notifications aimed at you — new customer added, shift approved / rejected, damage report, vacation request. Click one to jump straight to the record.

Mobile app

Every employee installs the Priya app on their phone (Android or iPhone). It gives them:

  • Today's shifts with turn-by-turn directions
  • GPS-verified clock in / out / break
  • Camera-first damage reports
  • Team chat with typing indicators
  • Vacation requests

You (as administrator) see the same schedule in the browser plus the org-wide dashboard tab in the mobile app.

14 · Your daily rhythm as administrator

Morning (15 min)

  • Open the Dashboard — confirm today's shifts and who checked in.
  • Any red "Pending" shifts? Ping the employee on Chat.
  • Review new customer sign-ups or damage reports overnight.

During the day (as needed)

  • Handle same-day cancellations — drag the shift to another employee or mark it as cancelled.
  • Approve completed shifts one by one, or in a batch at end of day.
  • Respond to Chat questions.

End of day (10 min)

  • Sweep the Billing approval queue to zero.
  • Post tomorrow's briefing in Team chat if anything is unusual.

End of month (30–45 min)

  • Monthly Lexware billing → Preview month → Generate now, then Lexware Sync.
  • Open the Alltagshilfe monthly report; create the manual insurer invoices in Lexware.
  • Follow up on any Overdue Priya invoices.

15 · Getting help

If something in the app looks wrong, or a customer / employee is missing, or a Lexware sync is stuck:

  1. Check the notification bell — many issues surface here first.
  2. Search for the customer on the Customer list — verify their status is Active, not Archived.
  3. Look in Settings → Audit log to see what was changed and by whom.

16 · Administrator cheat sheet — the whole flow on one page

This is the exact sequence for taking a brand-new customer from paperwork to a booked shift, and the sequence for onboarding a new employee. Keep this page open when you train someone new.

Adding a new client & their workplace

  1. Sidebar → Clients → New client.
  2. Pick service line: Priya's or Alltagshilfe.
  3. Fill the master-data wizard (§5 or §6).
  4. Save → land on the detail page.
  5. Properties tab → Add property (§8).
  6. Documents card → Upload file for contract + Pflegegradbescheid (§7).

Adding a new employee

  1. Sidebar → Employees → Invite employee.
  2. Fill master data — pick role, service line, employment type (§9).
  3. Save → the app emails an invitation (toast: "Employee created — invitation email sent.").
  4. Person clicks link, sets password, scans QR for 2FA.
  5. On their detail page: add skills, upload contract + Führungszeugnis (§10).
  6. Assign mandatory training videos — scheduling stays locked until watched.

Booking one shift on the timeline

  1. Sidebar → Schedule → Week view.
  2. Click empty cell at employee × day — the Plan shift dialog opens.
  3. Property (dropdown filtered to what the employee is qualified for) → Employee → Date → Start → End → Notes.
  4. Click Create shift. Toast: "Shift planned."
  5. Push notification goes to the employee's phone.

End-of-day approval

  1. Open Schedule → Billing approval.
  2. Each row: Property · Employee · Date · Scheduled · Actual · Billable (hrs).
  3. Approve when actual matches plan. Reject with a reason otherwise.
  4. Approved shifts lock and become billable.

End-of-month — Priya's regular clients

  1. Open Monthly Lexware billing.
  2. Preview month → review the dry-run summary.
  3. Generate now → creates one Draft per client with approved hours.
  4. Top-right Lexware Sync pushes all pending drafts → chip flips to Sent.
  5. Payment sync from Lexware later flips paid rows to Paid.

End-of-month — Alltagshilfe

  1. Sidebar → Reports → Alltagshilfe monthly report.
  2. Auto-generated month-end (banner: Auto-generated).
  3. Use it to create one manual invoice per insurer in Lexware.
