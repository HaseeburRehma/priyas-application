1 · Welcome & who this guide is for

This guide is written for the person who runs the operations of Priya's Reinigungsservice day-to-day — assigning employees to customers, tracking hours, and making sure every visit is invoiced correctly. It covers both service lines:

Priya's regular Cleaning contracts

Homes and companies that pay directly. Invoices go out automatically through Lexware.

Alltagshilfe Care support (§45b SGB XI)

Elderly customers billed to the care fund. Invoices are issued manually by your team, one per calendar month.

What just changed. The 30 Alltagshelfer customers from Priya's spreadsheet have been imported, each customer now has a document file cabinet, a payer-type chip is shown on the customer list, and there is a tablet-friendly intake wizard that captures the paper Kunden-Erfassungsbogen digitally.

2 · Signing in & the dashboard

  1. Open the app at https://priyas-application.vercel.app in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Sign in with your work email address and password. First-time users receive an invitation email; click the link and set your password.
  3. Two-factor code. On your first sign-in you'll be asked to scan a QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or 1Password). Every future sign-in asks for a 6-digit code.
  4. Landing on the dashboard. You'll see four KPI tiles at the top, today's shifts in the middle, and the recent activity feed on the right.

3 · Roles and what each one can do

Every person in the system has one of five roles. The role decides what they can see and change.

Admin

  • Everything below, plus:
  • Adding & removing users
  • Editing organisation settings
  • Approving invoices
  • Deleting records

Dispatcher

  • Creating customers & properties
  • Uploading documents
  • Scheduling shifts
  • Approving hours
  • Generating invoice drafts

Field staff

  • Their own shifts
  • GPS check-in / -out
  • Damage reports
  • Team chat
  • Vacation requests

Auditor

  • Read-only access to everything
  • Cannot change any data
  • Used for Priya's monthly compliance review

Customer contact

  • Sees only their own visits
  • Downloads their monthly invoice
  • Rarely used yet — for the customer-portal roll-out later this year
Rule of thumb. If a colleague reports "I can't see the schedule button" — check their role first. Field staff intentionally see only their own shifts, not the org-wide schedule.

7 · Uploading customer documents

Every customer now has a digital file cabinet — the paper folders your team keeps today can move into the app so anyone can find them in seconds.

  1. Open a customer. Scroll to the Documents card.
  2. Pick a category from the dropdown:
    • Contract — signed service agreement
    • Form — care-fund forms, tax forms
    • Decision — Pflegegradbescheid from the Krankenkasse
    • ID card — copy of the customer's ID or insurance card
    • Invoice — a copy of an outgoing bill
    • Photo — before / after photos, meter readings, key photos
    • Other — anything else
  3. Add an optional note — up to 80 characters.
  4. Click Upload file. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC, Word (doc / docx), Excel (xls / xlsx). Maximum 50 MB per file.
Privacy. Files are stored in a private storage bucket. The link that appears when you click Open is only valid for 60 seconds and cannot be forwarded — every viewer gets their own signed URL.

To view a document again later, click Open — it opens in a new tab. To remove one, click Delete (a manager permission).

8 · Adding properties (workplaces)

A property is the actual address where a visit happens. Most Alltagshilfe customers have a single property (their apartment). Companies often have several buildings under one contract.

  1. From the customer detail page, click Properties in the top tab bar, then Add property.
  2. Fill in:
    • Property name — e.g. "Onkenstraße 12 – EG links"
    • Address, PLZ, City
    • Kind — Apartment, House, Office, Commercial, Care home…
    • Weekly frequency — 1 for weekly, 0.5 for every two weeks
    • Access notes — key location, alarm code, parking, dog
  3. Save. The property is now bookable on the schedule.
Why properties matter. Shifts are always attached to a property, never to a customer directly. This means the app can show the field staff exactly where to go and, later, the GPS check-in verifies they arrived at the right address.

10 · Skills, qualifications, GPS check-in

On any employee's detail page you'll see three cards below the master data.

Skills & qualifications

Click Add skill. Common tags: window cleaning, industrial floor, car driver, bilingual DE/TR, bilingual DE/AR, certified Alltagshelfer §45a, Erste-Hilfe-Kurs. Skills feed the search box on the schedule — you can filter available staff by skill.

Training progress

Onboarding videos plus their completion state. New employees are locked out of scheduling until all mandatory videos are watched. You can waive individual videos with a manager note.

Documents

Same file cabinet as customers, scoped to the employee — contract, tax card (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung), Führungszeugnis, driver's licence copy, etc.

How GPS check-in works

  1. The employee opens the mobile app on their phone (Android or iPhone).
  2. They tap the day's shift → Clock in.
  3. The app captures their current GPS coordinates and compares them against the property's address.
  4. If they are within 300 metres, the shift starts. If not, the app asks them to confirm they are actually on site — the discrepancy is logged.
  5. Breaks and clock-out work the same way.

11 · Scheduling — the timeline

This is where you spend most of your day as an administrator.

  1. Click Schedule in the sidebar.
  2. The default view is the current week, employees down the left, days across the top. Each cell is one day.
  3. Switch view modes at the top:
    • Week — the operational planning view
    • Day — zoomed to today, with hourly detail
    • Month — bird's-eye view for spotting gaps
  4. Filter buttons above the grid narrow the list by service line (Priya's / Alltagshilfe), role, or skill.

Creating one shift

  1. Click an empty cell at the employee × day intersection you want.
  2. In the dialog, pick:
    • Customer — start typing the name; only customers assigned to your team appear
    • Property — auto-fills to the customer's only property, or offers a picker
    • Start time and duration
    • Break duration — 0 for short visits; 30 minutes for a 6-hour cleaning day
    • Repeat — one-off · every week · every two weeks · monthly, up to 12 occurrences
    • Notes — anything the employee needs to know that isn't already in the property notes
  3. Click Save shift. It appears on the grid as a coloured block — green for Priya's, red for Alltagshilfe.
  4. A push notification lands on the employee's phone; the shift also shows in their My Schedule tab.

Editing or moving a shift

  • Click a shift block to open its detail — change time, employee, or delete.
  • Drag a block to another cell to move it (the employee whose row it lands in becomes the new assignee).
  • If you move a repeating shift, the app asks whether to move only this one or the whole series.

Handling conflicts

If an employee is already booked at that time, or is on vacation, or hasn't completed their onboarding videos, the app blocks the save and tells you why. You can:

  • Pick a different employee
  • Move the earlier shift
  • Split the shift between two people
Repeating shifts save hours. Set an Alltagshilfe visit to "every two weeks, for 12 occurrences" once and you've booked the whole quarter. Editing one instance leaves the rest untouched.

12 · Approving hours & generating invoices

End of every shift

When the employee clocks out, the shift moves into Awaiting approval. You'll see a badge on the Schedule sidebar item — click into it and:

  1. Review the actual start / end / break times.
  2. If they match the plan, click Approve.
  3. If the employee ran over or short, click Adjust — enter the corrected duration and a short reason. The adjustment is audit-logged.
  4. Approved hours can no longer be edited by the field staff — they are locked and ready for billing.

End of every month — Priya's regular customers

  1. Click Invoices in the sidebar → Generate for month (bottom-right).
  2. Pick the month and confirm. The system creates one draft invoice per Priya customer who had approved shifts that month.
  3. Each draft is listed with a grey Draft chip. Click any draft to review the line items.
  4. Click Send — the invoice is pushed to Lexware, a PDF is emailed to the customer, and the chip turns amber Sent.
  5. Once the customer pays and Lexware syncs the payment back, the chip turns green Paid. Anything unpaid after 14 days automatically flips to red Overdue.

End of every month — Alltagshilfe customers

  1. Click Reports → Monthly report.
  2. Pick the month. The page groups approved visits by customer, by care fund, with hours and the €45b line.
  3. For each customer you can either Download PDF (for the care fund) or Email to management (an internal audit copy).
  4. Attach the report PDF to your usual manual Krankenkasse invoice. In a future release the invoice draft will be generated automatically from the same data.
Which colour means what? Grey = Draft (nothing sent yet). Amber = Sent (waiting for payment). Green = Paid. Red = Overdue (14 days after sent date).

13 · Chat, notifications & mobile app

Team chat

Click Chat in the sidebar. There's one #general channel plus channels per customer and per property. Post a message once, everyone with access to that scope sees it.

Notifications

The bell icon top-right shows all notifications aimed at you — new customer added, shift approved / rejected, damage report, vacation request. Click one to jump straight to the record.

Mobile app

Every employee installs the Priya app on their phone (Android or iPhone). It gives them:

  • Today's shifts with turn-by-turn directions
  • GPS-verified clock in / out / break
  • Camera-first damage reports
  • Team chat with typing indicators
  • Vacation requests

You (as administrator) see the same schedule in the browser plus the org-wide dashboard tab in the mobile app.

14 · Your daily rhythm as administrator

Morning (15 min)

  • Open the Dashboard — confirm today's shifts and who checked in.
  • Any red "Pending" shifts? Ping the employee on Chat.
  • Review new customer sign-ups or damage reports overnight.

During the day (as needed)

  • Handle same-day cancellations — drag the shift to another employee or mark it as cancelled.
  • Approve completed shifts one by one, or in a batch at end of day.
  • Respond to Chat questions.

End of day (10 min)

  • Sweep Awaiting approval queue to zero.
  • Post tomorrow's briefing in Chat if anything is unusual.

End of month (30–45 min)

  • Generate Priya's invoices → send.
  • Run Alltagshilfe monthly reports.
  • Pull payment status from Lexware and chase overdue.

15 · Getting help

If something in the app looks wrong, or a customer / employee is missing, or a Lexware sync is stuck:

  1. Check the notification bell — many issues surface here first.
  2. Search for the customer on the Customer list — verify their status is Active, not Archived.
  3. Look in Settings → Audit log to see what was changed and by whom.
  4. If none of that helps, email design@tylotech.de with a screenshot and the exact time the problem occurred.
