01Welcome + roles

Priya's is your one place for the whole cleaning operation — customers, buildings, staff, shifts, invoices, and communication. Every screen you'll ever need is one click away in the left sidebar.

Three roles

The app shows different things to different people, based on their role. Each user has exactly one role:

RoleWhoCan do
Management Priya, business partners Everything — settings, billing, team changes, reports
Project Manager Supervisors who run the schedule Manage customers + buildings, assign shifts, approve hours, view reports
Field Staff Cleaners on the ground See own schedule, clock in/out, log breaks, request vacation
Good to know If a menu item is missing on your screen, it's because your role doesn't have access. Ask a Management user to change your role if you need more.

02Signing in

Every day

  1. Open the web app in your browser. Bookmark the address so you don't have to type it every time.
  2. Type your email and password. Press Sign in.
  3. If you're a Management or Project Manager account, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
The sign-in screen. Type email + password, then press Sign in.

First time only — setting up 2FA (Management + Project Manager)

For safety, Management and Project Manager accounts must use two-factor authentication. It takes about 60 seconds and you only do it once.

  1. After your first sign-in you'll be sent to a page called "Secure your account with 2FA".
  2. On your phone, open the Google Authenticator app (free — install from App Store or Play Store if you don't have it). Any other authenticator app like 1Password or Authy also works.
  3. In the app, tap the "+" button, choose "Scan QR code", and point the camera at the QR shown on the screen.
  4. The app will now show a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds. Type the current code into the box and press Verify.
  5. You're done. Next time you sign in, the app will ask for a fresh 6-digit code from Authenticator.
Keep it safe If you lose your phone, contact support to reset your 2FA. Don't turn 2FA off — Management accounts control the money.

Forgot your password?

  1. On the sign-in page, click Forgot your password?.
  2. Type your email address and press Send reset link.
  3. Check your inbox. Click the link, choose a new password, and sign in again.

04Clients (customers)

A client is anyone who pays for cleaning. Each client can have one address (for residential) or many buildings (for commercial). This is the starting point for everything else — you can't create an invoice or plan a shift without a client first.

Seeing the customer list

  1. Click Clients in the left sidebar.
  2. The list shows every client with their status, contact, and the number of buildings.
  3. Use the search box at the top to find a specific one by name.
  4. Filter by status (Active / Vacation / Paused / Contract ending / Inactive) using the pills across the top.

Adding a new client

  1. On the Clients page, click the green + New client button in the top-right.
  2. Choose the customer type:
    • Residential — a private household
    • Commercial — a business with one or more locations
    • Alltagshilfe — a care recipient billed to a German health-insurance fund (§45b SGB XI)
  3. Fill in the client details step by step. Fields with a red asterisk are required — the app will not let you skip them.
  4. For Alltagshilfe, also fill in: care level (Pflegegrad 1–5), health insurance name, and the assignment agreement (Abtretungserklärung) date.
  5. Choose invoice export target: Internal means Priya's manages the invoice inside the app, Lexware means invoices push automatically to your accounting.
  6. Press Save client.
Step 1 of the New Client wizard — pick the customer type.

Setting a client's status

On the client detail page, use the status dropdown to reflect what's really happening:

  • Active — normal ongoing service.
  • Vacation — client is away; shifts paused temporarily.
  • Paused by client — client has asked to stop for now.
  • Contract ending in 60 days — final notice given.
  • Inactive — no longer a customer.
Why status matters Vacation and Paused hide the client from the "Today's shifts" auto-planner so you don't accidentally send a cleaner to an empty house.

Editing a client

  1. Open the client from the list.
  2. Press Edit at the top-right of the detail page.
  3. Change anything you need. Press Save.

05Properties (buildings)

A property is a physical building or address where cleaning happens. One client can have many properties (a company with three offices), or just one (a private house).

Adding a property under a client

  1. Open the client detail page.
  2. Scroll down to the Properties section.
  3. Click + Add property.
  4. Fill in:
    • Name — e.g. "Head office" or "Wilmersdorf apartment"
    • Street, postal code, city — used for GPS check-in on the mobile app
    • Access instructions — key code, buzzer, parking notes
    • Cleaning rhythm — how often (weekly, bi-weekly, on demand)
  5. Press Save.
Why the address matters When a cleaner clocks in from their phone, the app checks their GPS location against the property's address. If they're more than 500 metres away, it warns them before letting them proceed. Get the address right the first time.

Photos, keys, and documents

On the property detail page you can:

  • Upload photos of the entrance, key handover, or damage.
  • Track physical keys — who has them, when they were handed over.
  • Attach contract PDFs, safety checklists, or property maps.

06Schedule

The Schedule page is where you decide who cleans what, and when.

Assigning a shift

  1. Click Schedule in the sidebar.
  2. Switch to Week or Day view depending on how far ahead you're planning.
  3. Click an empty slot on the calendar (or click + New shift).
  4. Choose the property, the employee, the start and end times.
  5. Optionally set it as recurring (every week, every two weeks) so you don't have to redo it.
  6. Press Assign.
Week view of the schedule. Green blocks = Priya's shifts. Click any empty area to add a new one.

Approving completed shifts

After a cleaner clocks out, the shift moves to Awaiting approval. As Project Manager or Management:

  1. Open the shift.
  2. Check the actual check-in / check-out times against the schedule.
  3. Press Approve. This unlocks the hours for invoicing.
Important Only approved shifts can be included in an invoice. If you can't find hours in the invoice generator, check whether the shifts are still awaiting approval.

07Employees

Adding a new employee

  1. Sidebar → Settings → Team & roles → + Invite member.
  2. Enter their email, full name, and pick a role: Management Project Manager Field Staff
  3. Press Send invite. The person gets an email with a link to set their password.
  4. Once they've signed in, open the Employees page to fill in the rest: phone, weekly hours target, hire date, certifications, spoken languages.

Onboarding video sequence

New Field Staff must watch a mandatory training video series before the app unlocks for them. They're bounced to the Onboarding videos page automatically on first sign-in.

Certificates + expiry alerts

On the employee detail page, upload copies of first-aid certificate, work permit, or driving licence. Set an expiry date and the app will send a notification 30 days before it lapses.

08Creating invoices

The most-asked question: can I bill one customer for all their buildings at once, or should I make one invoice per building? The answer is both — you choose per invoice.

Combined bill — all buildings on one invoice

Use this when the customer prefers one big invoice per month.

  1. Sidebar → Invoices → + New invoice (top-right).
  2. Choose the client.
  3. Choose the date range (e.g. 1–30 June).
  4. Leave the property selector as All buildings.
  5. The app draws every approved shift for every building of that client in the period, and groups the line items by building.
  6. Press Save draft.

Separate bills — one invoice per building

Use this when each building is a separate cost centre or has its own invoice recipient.

  1. Same steps 1–3 as above.
  2. In the property selector, pick just one building.
  3. Save the draft, then repeat for the next building.
Which one does Priya want by default? The system remembers what you picked last time for that client. If you always want combined, always leave the selector on "All buildings" and it becomes the pattern.

Editing a draft

Every invoice starts as Draft. Only drafts can be edited.

  1. Open the invoice from the list.
  2. Press Edit invoice at the top of the detail page.
  3. Change line item quantity, unit price, or description. Add or remove lines.
  4. Change the invoice date, due date, or add a note to the customer.
  5. Press Save. The totals recalculate automatically.
Once you press "Mark Sent" you cannot edit any more Because at that point the invoice is a legal document that has been shared with the customer. Double-check everything before you send.

Sending to Lexware

If the client has Lexware as their export target, an extra button appears once the invoice is marked sent:

  1. Open the sent invoice.
  2. Press Lexware-Sync jetzt (Sync to Lexware now).
  3. The invoice pushes to Lexware Office. You'll see a green Synced chip once it lands.

To sync many at once: on the Invoices list, click Sync Lexware in the toolbar. Anything Failed or Pending gets retried in one go.

Marking as paid

Two ways:

  • Manually — open the invoice, press Mark paid, enter the amount and date.
  • Automatically from Lexware — press Lexware-Zahlungen abrufen (Pull payments) on any invoice. The app checks Lexware for any new payments and books them against the matching invoices.

Overdue chasing

The system automatically flips invoices to Overdue the day after the due date. On the Invoices list, filter by Overdue to see everything past due, oldest first. A reminder email is sent automatically 3 days after due date if the setting is on (Settings → Tax & billing → Late-payment reminder).

09Monthly report (Alltagshilfe)

For Alltagshilfe customers, invoices go to the customer's health-insurance fund, not the customer directly. The app generates a monthly report you use to create those invoices manually.

Reading the report

  1. Sidebar → Reports → Monthly Report.
  2. Use the month pills (Jan / Feb / Mar / Apr) at the top to switch periods.
  3. The report shows every Alltagshilfe customer as a pink header row, with each cleaner's hours grouped underneath.
  4. Total hours, total amount, and per-customer breakdown all update automatically.

Sending to management

Press Send to Management in the toolbar. An email with the PDF report goes to the address in Settings → Company. This is what you or Priya use to create the manual invoices for each health-insurance fund.

Why not automatic? German health-insurance invoices need to be tailored per fund (their format, addressee, and reference numbers differ). The app does the heavy lifting — gathering hours, calculating amounts — you finish the last step manually so the paperwork is exactly what each Kasse expects.

10Chat + notifications

Team chat

  1. Sidebar → Team chat.
  2. You'll see all channels you're a member of (usually one per team or one per property).
  3. Click a channel, type a message, press Send.
  4. Attach a shift or property card to a message using the paperclip icon — good for "Can someone cover this?" moments.

Notifications

Sidebar → Notifications shows every alert the system generated for you: shift changes, invoice paid, missed check-in, vacation request, etc. Filter with the pills at the top. Bell icon in the topbar shows a red dot when there's something unread.

Turn categories on/off in Settings → Notifications. Each category has 3 channels: in-app, email, SMS/WhatsApp.

12Common questions

How do I see who's on shift today?

Dashboard → the "Today's schedule" card on the right shows every shift in time order. Click a row for details.

How do I see what's overdue?

Sidebar → Invoices → click the Overdue filter pill. Sorted oldest first.

How do I export a list to Excel?

Most list pages (Clients, Invoices, Employees, Reports) have an Export button in the toolbar. Downloads a CSV file which opens directly in Excel.

How do I reset an employee's password?

Ask them to click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. If they can't access their email, Management can go to Settings → Team & roles, click the person's row, and press Send password reset.

Why can't I see the Settings menu?

Only Management + Project Manager roles see most Settings. Field Staff sees only My account, Security, and Sessions.

Why can't I edit this invoice?

It's already been marked Sent. Only drafts can be edited. If something needs fixing on a sent invoice, create a credit note or a corrected invoice.

The QR code on 2FA setup doesn't scan

Below the QR is a text code (a long string like JBSWY3DPEHPK3PXP). In your Authenticator app, choose "Enter setup key" instead of "Scan QR" and paste the code. Same effect.

I made a mistake — can I undo?

Most edits create a version in the audit log. Ask Management to check Settings → Audit log to see what changed and when. Deleted clients / properties can usually be un-archived within 30 days.
